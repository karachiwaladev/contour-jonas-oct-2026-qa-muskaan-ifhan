## 🧭 Verdict (Triage Tess): **fix first**

There are two majors: data loss across tabs, and editing that's unreachable without a mouse.
There's no blocker, so the app works for a single-tab mouse user, but it shouldn't be called
done.

| # | Severity | Finding | Area | Personas | Root cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **major** | Editing in one tab is **overwritten by a stale save from another tab** | persistence | Cathy | Each tab keeps its own in-memory copy; `db.update` writes the whole stale object back (`put`) |
| 2 | **major** | **Editing can't be discovered or reached by keyboard** | edit, a11y | Nora, Sadie | Editing is bound only to `dblclick` on a non-focusable `<span>`; the only hint is a hover tooltip |
| 3 | **major** (a11y) | **Focus is lost after the list re-renders**: delete, or Esc in the editor | a11y | Sadie, Sam | `render()` replaces every row and never puts focus back |
| 4 | minor | Two submits in the same tick **create a duplicate task** | add | Sam | The title is read before `await db.add`, and nothing guards a submit already in flight |
| 5 | polish | "**Clear** completed" **deletes** permanently; neither confirm says it can't be undone | copy | Lena (and Carl's note) | The label and confirm wording understate permanence |

### 1. Stale cross-tab save loses an edit (major)
- **Steps:**
  1. Open the app in tabs A and B.
  2. In A, add "Buy milk".
  3. Reload B; it shows "Buy milk".
  4. In A, double-click it and rename it to "Buy oat milk".
  5. In B, which still shows "Buy milk", tick the task.
  6. Reload A.
- **Expected:** "Buy oat milk", ticked.
- **Actual:** "Buy milk". A's rename is gone.
- **Evidence:** `personas-run.js` → `cathy.tabBShowsStale: true`, `afterRaceTitles: ["Buy milk"]`.
- **Fix idea:** update only the changed fields on a freshly read record, and refresh other tabs through a `BroadcastChannel`.

### 2. Editing is undiscoverable and keyboard-unreachable (major)
- **Steps:** look at a task row; Tab through the page; focus a title and press Enter, F2 or Space.
- **Expected:** a visible way to edit that works from the keyboard.
- **Actual:** the row shows only the title, chips and "×". "Double-click to edit" appears only as a tooltip, and the word "edit" appears nowhere on the page. Titles aren't tab stops, and no key opens the editor.
- **Evidence:** the MCP snapshot (uid 14_45 is static text with no control); `sadie.titleIsTabStop: false`, `editReachableByKeyboard: false`.
- **Fix idea:** a labelled Edit button per row (`aria-label="Edit 'X'"`) that opens the existing editor.

### 3. Focus lost after delete or Esc (major for keyboard and screen-reader users)
- **Steps:** Tab to a "Delete" button, press Enter and confirm. Separately, open the editor and press Esc.
- **Expected:** focus moves to the next task, or back to the task that was edited.
- **Actual:** `document.activeElement` is `BODY`.
- **Evidence:** `sadie.focusAfterDelete: "BODY (lost)"`; Sam: `focusAfterEsc: "BODY"`.
- **Fix idea:** remember the focused row's index before `render()`, then focus the same or next row's checkbox.

### 4. Same-tick double submit duplicates a task (minor)
- **Steps:** with text in the box, submit twice in one tick (a bouncy or held Enter key).
- **Expected:** one task.
- **Actual:** two. 30 ms apart is fine.
- **Evidence:** Sam: `doubleEnterSameTick: 2`, `doubleEnter30ms: 1`.
- **Fix idea:** clear the input before `await db.add`, or ignore a submit while one is still in flight.

### 5. "Clear completed" wording (polish)
- **Actual:** the button says *Clear*, the confirm says `Delete 1 completed task?`, and the single-delete confirm says `Delete "X"?`. Nothing says it's permanent.
- **Fix idea:** label the button "Delete completed" and add "This can't be undone" to both confirms.
